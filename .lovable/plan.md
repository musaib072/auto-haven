

# Car Marketplace App

A sleek car trading marketplace where users can browse, list, and explore cars for sale — built with sample/demo data.

## Pages

### 1. Home Page
- Hero section with search bar (make, model, price range)
- Featured/trending car listings grid
- Quick category filters (SUV, Sedan, Truck, etc.)
- Call-to-action for selling a car

### 2. Browse Listings
- Filterable grid of car cards (make, model, year, price, mileage, location)
- Sidebar filters: price range, year, mileage, body type, fuel type, transmission
- Sort by: price, newest, mileage
- Pagination

### 3. Car Detail Page
- Image gallery/carousel
- Full specs (engine, transmission, color, features)
- Price and seller info card with "Contact Seller" button
- Similar listings section

### 4. Sell Your Car Page
- Multi-step form: car details → photos → pricing → review
- Preview of how the listing will look

### 5. About / How It Works
- Simple info page explaining the buying/selling process

## Design
- Clean, modern dark/light UI with automotive feel
- Card-based listings with hover effects
- Responsive for mobile and desktop
- ~15-20 sample car listings with realistic data
